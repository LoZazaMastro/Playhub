The Playhub addition independently implements these protocol facts through its
existing verified WMI DSTS provider: CPU0x00120094/GPU0x00120097; upper16bits
0x0001, low16bits integer Celsius1..125. Zero/unavailable is not a temperature.
No Panel implementation, ATKACPI transport, upstream service, writer, driver or
third-party binary is copied or redistributed by this addition. The existing
Playhub/G-Helper fan reference and fan behavior remain separate and unchanged.
Physical RC73XA/provider validation is still required; mocked tests do not prove
real hardware or firmware support.
