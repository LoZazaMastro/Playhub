Source commit: 42f6bdeadde3a9526dc8eb713763476999ac217b
https://github.com/legendary-gl/legendary/releases/tag/0.21.1

Heroic gogdl 1.4.0, imLinguin / Heroic contributors, GPL-3.0.
Source commit: 6c6ef53fd4965d8d9b943c73d9400fe55eadc6ba
https://github.com/Heroic-Games-Launcher/heroic-gogdl/releases/tag/v1.4.0
Its xdelta submodule source at 0525275fe4b553a10f38e455d30c60dc6ed9b45d
is included separately (Apache-2.0).

Exact upstream source archives, build workflows and dependency lockfiles are
provided in CorrespondingSource. Additional locked dependency source distributions
and their license notices are included. CPython PSF terms and third-party notices,
PyInstaller GPL terms with its distribution exception, requests/dependency notices,
PyCryptodome and optional webview dependency notices are in Notices.

The stock binaries bundle their own Python runtime; no Python, Heroic, Epic Games
Launcher or GOG Galaxy installation is required for these download providers.
Dependency versions in source-provenance.json describe the locked build inputs;
the exact embedded stock-binary dependency SBOM has not been reconstructed.

Playhub's Windows queue, IPC, supervisor and UI are original MIT-licensed code.
Unifideck (mubaraknumann/unifideck, GPL-3.0-or-later) is the source of the
Epic and GOG achievement-provider adaptations. Their source provenance,
Windows changes and full license are retained in Notices/Unifideck-0.8.0-NOTICE.txt
and Notices/GPL-3.0.txt. No Winhanced source or artwork is incorporated here.
