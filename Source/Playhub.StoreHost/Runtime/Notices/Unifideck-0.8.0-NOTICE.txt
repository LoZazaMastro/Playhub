StoreCliAchievements.cs and StoreAchievementNormalization.cs adapt the Epic
GraphQL operations, GOG Galaxy game-token/achievement flow, and normalization
from stores/epic/achievements.py, stores/gog/achievements.py and galaxy_api.py.
Windows adaptation and cache integration: Copyright (c) 2026 LoZazaMastro.
These adapted files retain GPL-3.0-or-later headers. Original MIT files retain
their own notices. The combined distribution includes these GPL components.

The Windows transport validates certificates, refuses redirects, bounds all
responses and pagination, and keeps account tokens in the native store broker.
The Linux reference's permissive TLS fallback and shell token arguments are
not used. This notice does not relicense third-party store services or assets.
