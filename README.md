## Digital Stores, from your controller

<img src="docs/images/readme-card-digital-stores-steam.png?v=300-readme-current" width="100%" alt="Browse, install and manage Epic Games, GOG, Xbox Store, PC Game Pass and Amazon Games in Steam Big Picture, with a shared download queue, artwork and Xbox, Epic and GOG achievements" />

Connect your accounts once in the Playhub app, then open **Steam menu > Digital Stores** in Big Picture. For the PC Game Pass catalog, press **Y** (Triangle on a PlayStation controller), choose **Xbox Game Pass** in Filters, and close the filters panel.

Direct installation, language choices and download controls depend on the store and title. PC Game Pass titles requiring EA app, Ubisoft Connect or Battle.net are labelled with their launcher and do not yet support direct installation. Add supported, externally installed titles through **Import Games**. Amazon Games does not have an achievement system.

## Steam Controller 2, across your games

<img src="docs/images/readme-card-controller.png?v=300-readme-current" width="100%" alt="Use Steam Controller 2 in non-Steam games while Steam stays open, without setting up a separate controller utility. Enable Translation Controller in the Controller tab of the Playhub Quick Access plugin." />

If a game already recognises your controller and you get double inputs, turn **Translation Controller** off in the Playhub plugin's **Controller** tab.

## Download and installation

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/LoZazaMastro/Playhub/releases/latest);
2. Download `Playhub-Setup.exe`;
3. Run the installer, choose your language, and select **Install**;
4. On first launch, let Playhub check Steam, Decky, and Gaming Mode.

Playhub installs for the current Windows user. Windows 11 x64 is recommended; on Windows 10, the app uses a solid dark theme instead of acrylic effects.
